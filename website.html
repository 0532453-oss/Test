FAQ

Got questions?

How long does it take to set up FlexDesk?
Most teams are up and running in under 30 minutes. Import your existing data, invite your team, and start scheduling jobs right away. Our onboarding wizard guides you through every step.
Can I try FlexDesk before committing?
Absolutely! We offer a 14-day free trial with full access to all Professional plan features. No credit card required. If you need more time, just reach out.
Does FlexDesk work offline?
Yes. Our mobile app uses offline-first technology (IndexedDB sync) so your technicians can view job details, update statuses, and capture signatures even without internet. Everything syncs when connectivity returns.
What integrations do you support?
FlexDesk integrates with Stripe, QuickBooks, Google Calendar, Slack, WhatsApp, Outlook, Twilio, Zapier, and more. Our API is fully documented for custom integrations.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. No long-term contracts, no cancellation fees. Your data remains accessible for 30 days after cancellation.
Is my data secure?
FlexDesk uses enterprise-grade security with AES-256 encryption, workspace-scoped row-level security, JWT authentication, and regular security audits. We're SOC 2 Type II compliant.
