# FlexDesk CRM - User Manual

FlexDesk is a customer relationship management (CRM) platform designed for field service professionals like HVAC technicians, plumbers, electricians, and landscapers.

## Getting Started

### Accessing the App

Navigate to your FlexDesk deployment URL in your web browser.

### Signing In

You have two options to sign in:

#### Option 1: Email & Password
1. Enter your email address
2. Enter your password
3. Click **Sign in**

#### Option 2: Sign in with Google (Recommended)
1. Click **Sign in with Google**
2. Select your Google account
3. Grant FlexDesk permission to access your email and profile
4. You'll be automatically redirected back and logged in

> **Note**: If this is your first time signing in with Google, a workspace will be automatically created for you.

---

## Dashboard Overview

After signing in, you'll land on your Dashboard which provides:

- **Client Count**: Total number of active clients
- **Job Count**: Number of scheduled/active jobs
- **Revenue**: Total revenue for the current period
- **Upcoming Jobs**: List of your next scheduled appointments
- **Quick Actions**: Shortcuts to common tasks

---

## Core Features

### 1. Client Management

#### Adding a New Client
1. Click **Add New Client** from the dashboard or navigate to **Clients**
2. Fill in the client details:
   - Full Name / Business Name
   - Email Address
   - Phone Number
   - Service Address
   - Notes (optional)
3. Click **Save**

#### Viewing Client Details
- Click on any client to view their full profile
- See job history, invoices, and contact information
- Edit or update client information as needed

### 2. Job Scheduling

#### Creating a New Job
1. Navigate to **Jobs** or click **Schedule a Job**
2. Select the client
3. Choose the service type
4. Set the date and time
5. Add job notes or special instructions
6. Click **Create Job**

#### Job Calendar
- View all scheduled jobs in calendar format
- Drag and drop to reschedule
- Color-coded by job status (scheduled, in-progress, completed)

### 3. Invoicing

#### Creating an Invoice
1. Navigate to **Invoices** or click **Create Invoice**
2. Select the client
3. Add line items (services, parts, labor)
4. Set payment terms
5. Click **Send Invoice**

#### Invoice Status
- **Draft**: Not yet sent
- **Sent**: Delivered to client
- **Paid**: Payment received
- **Overdue**: Past due date
