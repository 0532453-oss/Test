Multi-Tenant Architecture

Every query scoped to the workspace. Zero data leakage between tenants with Prisma RLS.

Offline-First Engine

IndexedDB queues mutations. Auto-syncs on reconnect. Works in basements and dead zones.

AI-Powered Agent

Twilio voice + SMS. Books appointments, answers questions, follows up — 24/7.

Accordion
Smooth expand/collapse for FAQ content.
The AI Agent uses Twilio for voice and SMS integration. It maintains conversation context, understands HVAC-specific terminology, and can book appointments directly into Google Calendar. It's trained on your service catalog and pricing.
Yes. The mobile app uses IndexedDB to queue all changes locally. When connectivity returns, the sync manager reconciles with the server automatically. Job updates, photos, and signatures all work offline.
Every database query is scoped to the authenticated workspace via Prisma's RLS extension. The middleware injects workspace context from the JWT, ensuring no data can leak between tenants — even in complex joins.
FlexDesk integrates with Stripe for checkout sessions and recurring billing. Invoices are auto-generated as PDFs with your branding. The AI agent sends payment reminders and can take payment confirmations over the phone.
